## About

WaCRM is a self-hosted CRM built for WhatsApp Business. Your team gets a shared inbox for WhatsApp messages alongside contact records and deal pipelines. Send broadcasts, manage conversations, and track sales.

WaCRM runs as a TypeScript app that connects to the WhatsApp Business API. Deploy from this template, connect your WhatsApp Business account, and your team can manage all customer conversations from one dashboard.
