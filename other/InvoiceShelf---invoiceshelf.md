## About

InvoiceShelf is an open-source invoicing app for freelancers and small businesses. Create professional invoices, track payments, manage expenses, and generate reports. Self-hosted so your financial data stays on your server.

InvoiceShelf runs as a PHP app with a web dashboard. Deploy from this template and manage your invoicing from any browser. Supports multiple currencies, tax rates, recurring invoices, and client portals.
