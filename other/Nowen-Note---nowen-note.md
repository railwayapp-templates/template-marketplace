## About

Nowen Note is a self-hosted notebook app with markdown and rich text editing, AI-assisted writing, mind maps, and full-text search. Your notes stay on your server instead of Notion or Evernote.

Nowen Note runs as a TypeScript app with a built-in web server. Deploy from this template and access your notes from any device through a browser. Organize notes into nested notebooks, tag them, and search across everything.
