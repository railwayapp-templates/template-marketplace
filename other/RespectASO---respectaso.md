## About

RespectASO is a free app store optimization keyword research tool. It scrapes keyword data from the App Store and Google Play, shows you search volume and difficulty, and helps you pick terms that real people search for. No paid API keys needed.

RespectASO runs as a Python web app with a built-in interface. Deploy from this template and run keyword research whenever you want without paying for a SaaS tool. Everything runs on your Railway instance.
