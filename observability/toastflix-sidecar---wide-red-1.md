## About

Deploy your own private instance of Toastflix Sidecar with a single click on Railway.

Toastflix Sidecar is an automated container service engineered to handle media resolution, extraction, and proxy tasks with minimal overhead. Hosting it on Railway provides a fully managed deployment pipeline that provisions resources, handles continuous delivery from your source code, and binds directly to a secure public HTTPS endpoint without manual server provisioning.
