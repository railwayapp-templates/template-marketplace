## About

DEEIX-Chat puts Claude, GPT, Gemini, and other models behind one chat interface. Pick the right model for each conversation. Track what each person on your team spends. One deployment, every model, one bill.

Go binary with SQLite. Single service, no database needed. Dockerfile in the repo. Handles authentication, per-user billing, file uploads, and tool use.
