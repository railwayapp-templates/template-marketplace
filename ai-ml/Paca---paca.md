## About

Paca is an AI-native project board for teams where humans and AI agents work side by side. Agents pick up tasks, write code, and move cards across the board.

Paca runs as a single Go binary. Deploy from this template and your team gets a project board in any browser. Sprints, backlogs, kanban columns, and AI agent integration through its API.
