## About

[Image to Video AI](https://imagetovideoai.pro/) is an AI image-to-video generator that turns still images into short, story-driven clips with natural motion, realistic physics, first-and-last-frame control, optional voiceover, and multi-model workflows for fast creative iteration. This Railway template deploys a minimal public web starter that introduces Image to Video AI and sends visitors to the main Image to Video AI site.

This template is designed as a simple, safe starting point for image-to-video AI demos, visual content workflow documentation, product demo routing, or internal proof-of-concept pages. It uses a small Node.js Express server, reads Railway's standard `PORT` environment variable, and serves a polished page with a clear call to action. No API keys, private application files, or customer data are included.
